## What changed architecturally

- **Stable-slot chunked storage (ADR-0001).** Copy-on-write chunks, with tombstones instead of compaction. Kept faces keep their IDs, which is what makes selections survive edits cheaply. Every chunk carries its own cached hash, so a revision hashes only what it copied.
- **`MeshEdit` is the single edit path.** It records provenance as it goes: removed, created (with sources), modified, moved, and slot remaps for compaction.
- **History is a DAG** of document states. Undo and redo move a cursor, and abandoned branches are kept. Only the current revision keeps derived topology.
- **Selections are data,** translated through provenance up to the common ancestor and back down. Loops match on directed edges and components resolve individually.
- **Replay verdicts are strict.** `Exact` means the bytes are equal. `Equivalent` means the structure is equal (slots, liveness, indices and orientation) with positions within tolerance. Inputs are verified before each command.

## Independent review

Verdict: "with fixes". 1 Critical and 9 Important findings, all fixed in one pass, each with a test that failed first:
- **C1:** dangling face after a vertex created and removed in the same edit
- **I1–I3:** selection translation correctness
- **I4:** `Equivalent` too weak (no structure or orientation check, input unchecked)
- **I5:** history kept every revision's topology (3.2× instead of 1.5×)
- **I6:** per-command full-mesh hashing and summary (about 3.5 s at 5M)
- **I7:** pinched pairing was geometry-only
- **I8:** quadratic edit bookkeeping (15.5 s on a 100k-move test)
- **I9:** provenance couldn't express compaction or modified faces

The minor findings are tracked as MF-ISSUE-021…027.

## Found along the way

- **Intermittent Linux CI segfault.** Mesa's lavapipe Vulkan driver crashes when wgpu devices are created and destroyed concurrently. Scratch-branch stress runs ruled out two hypotheses, reproduced it at 3 in 15 with `WGPU_BACKEND=vulkan`, and verified the fix (one shared headless device per process) at 0 in 30.
- **serde_json's default float parsing is best-effort.** A command record failed to round-trip about 1 run in 5 (0.171417 came back as 0.17141699999999999). `float_roundtrip` is now enabled workspace-wide, because log parameters must be exact.
- **Export verification had been re-importing** (1 GB peak at 5M). It's now structural for STL and streaming for OBJ.
